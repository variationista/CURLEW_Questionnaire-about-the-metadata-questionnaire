Help shape the CURLEW questionnaire

We are deciding what kinds of information CURLEW should collect and how best to collect them. This consultation is organised by broad themes rather than finished survey questions.

For each topic, please indicate how relevant you think it is, which methods you would consider, and any wording, response options or methodological concerns you would like the team to discuss. You may select more than one method. There are no required answers, and you do not need a strong view on every topic.

Your responses are recommendations, not final decisions. Each team member's contribution will inform the collective discussion. Some subcategories are potential extensions beyond the current questionnaire and are included for consideration.
